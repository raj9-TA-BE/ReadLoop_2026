Atomic Habits

James Clear
Habits Productivity Psychology
CORE ESSENCE

Tiny 1% improvements compound into remarkable results over time. Forget goals — build systems instead. Every action you take is a vote for the identity you want to become. The habit loop (cue, craving, response, reward) can be redesigned with four laws: make it obvious, make it attractive, make it easy, and make it satisfying. Your environment shapes your behaviour more than your willpower ever will.

"You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems."

3 LESSONS TO ACT ON TODAY

1
Identity-based habits outlast motivation-based ones — ask who you want to be, not what you want to achieve.
2
Environment design beats willpower — put your book on your pillow, not on a shelf.
3
Never miss twice — the recovery habit is the real habit that defines you.
